# Neon Slip: modular boss fights — 2026-11-08

Added boss.js and integrated The Warden into the Arcade edition. The encounter director makes exactly one independent 25% roll at each crossed 1,000-score milestone, including score jumps and points earned during combat. Successful rolls queue; the current normal wave finishes, new normal waves stop, and the boss enters safely. Restart resets the director. All new combat state is transient and existing save keys remain intact.

Each fight chooses four unique modules from eight weapons (scatter, rail, rockets, mortar, saw, arc fence, helix and drones), with independent module health, distinct artwork and attack behavior. Destroying modules stops future attacks from them; a destroyed rail module cancels its beam. All four modules must be destroyed to expose the core, which has its own final attack. Rockets and drones can be shot down. Attacks have telegraphs and share rendered/collision geometry with swept projectile checks.

One of three temporary player guns (Twin Pulse, Burst Driver or Ion Lance) auto-fires during active combat, with a visible cannon attachment on the ship. Steer underneath modules to aim. The fight grants a three-point temporary combat hull and full Dash charge; existing shields take priority and Dash phases through attacks. Core defeat clears attacks immediately, awards +400 score (+60 per module), counts one cleared encounter, refills Dash, removes the gun, and safely resumes the route after the victory animation. Pause freezes combat and firing; defeat/restart hide or clear the combat display/state.

Updated flight.css for the boss HUD, index.html for encounter integration and accessible controls, game details, What's New, update notes, and the catalog fingerprint. Classic remains unchanged. Temporary deterministic boss/compact-screen review pages were removed before packaging.
