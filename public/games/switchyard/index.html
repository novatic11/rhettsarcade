A DISPATCHER’S POCKET GUIDE

Keep the little trains moving.

Each train carries a color and a letter: A Rosebay, B Sunfield, C Pinecross, D Blueport. Send it to the matching station.

  1. Switch 1 sends trains toward the A/B pair or the C/D pair. Switch 2 chooses A or B; Switch 3 chooses C or D. A train takes the route selected when it reaches a junction.
  2. A junction marked CHECK needs a flip for a train. Hover or focus a train to trace its current route. The next three arrivals appear on the board. Rush Hour includes striped express trains, which move faster and earn +75 points. Practice has slower trains and unlimited strikes; it does not change high scores.
  3. Marked crossings share rails. A red ring and warning sound mean two trains are on course to meet. Keep trains apart there. Tap a train (or its chip below) to hold it for two seconds, once per train. Tap a held train again to release it early. Trains on the same track brake automatically.
  4. Use Slow time or S for four seconds of breathing room. Every six correct deliveries earns a charge, up to two.

Correct deliveries build a score multiplier. A wrong station or a crossing collision uses one of three strikes and resets your streak. After 90 seconds, finish clearing the trains already on the board. Bronze: 1,200 · Silver: 2,400 · Gold: 4,000. Medals and best scores are saved separately for each board and ranked shift. Achievements unlock train paint; destination colors and letters always stay visible. Link your arcade save password to carry progress between devices.
