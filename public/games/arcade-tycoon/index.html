    Running an arcade

    1. Build. Pick an attraction and click the floor. Visitors need a clear tile beside every machine, and the entrance must stay open.
    2. Open the doors. Visitors arrive with a budget and their own tastes. Kids love prize games, teens love speed and rhythm, adults love the classics, collectors chase tickets.
    3. Keep them happy. Visitors get hungry, thirsty, tired and need the restroom. Long lines, broken machines and litter all hurt. Decor makes nearby machines more inviting.
    4. Grow. Upgrade machines (up to level 5), hire staff, buy improvements and run campaigns. Hit the goals in Business → Location to move into six bigger venues, each with new attractions.

    A day lasts 90 seconds while the arcade is busy. Rent, wages and power are paid at the end of each day. Random events can shake things up. Progress saves automatically; time stops while you’re away.
