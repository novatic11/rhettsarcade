WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBORHOOD

Your arcade starts here.

  1. Build. Choose a machine and click an empty floor tile. Keep the entrance and the tile in front of each machine clear.
  2. Open. Customers walk in, play, and pay you. A visitor can try up to three different attractions. Their favorites boost happiness; snacks satisfy hunger. Benches and a floor host help them wait in queues.
  3. Grow. Click machines to upgrade or repair them. Unlock attractions with paid plays. Grow through five locations, then build the Grand Arcade with all eight attraction types. Zoom in to arrange a large floor. Hire staff to fix machines and welcome customers.

A day lasts 90 seconds while your arcade is running. Rent starts at $20 per day and rises with room size. Staff wages are added to daily costs. Closing the doors stops new visitors. Progress saves automatically. Link a password in the launcher’s Your saves to continue on another device. Time stops while you’re away. Claim goal rewards in Staff & business, run flyer campaigns, and improve your whole arcade.
