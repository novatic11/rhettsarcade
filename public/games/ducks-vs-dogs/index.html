 THE PONDKEEPER’S FIELD GUIDE

Feathers before footprints.

Choose a duck card, then tap a garden square. Dogs walk in from the right and chew through your defenders. Campaign teaches a new duck in every level with fixed enemy waves. Endless starts with the full flock and has no final wave.

  1. Keep crumbs coming. Your starting crumbs depend on the level and difficulty; lesson steps always stay affordable. Campaign difficulty changes the attacking dogs, not your ducks. Endless keeps its original balance. Passive crumbs arrive every 7 seconds once the waves start: 25 on Easy or the original balance, 20 on Normal, and 16 on Hard. Breadwinners bake another 25 every 9 seconds. Crumbs are collected automatically.
  2. Build by lane. Shooters fire only to their right. Put Breadwinners near the pond on the left, shooters behind guards, and watch the incoming lane counts below. Card cooldowns keep powerful ducks from being spammed.
  3. Give the goose a chance. Each lane has one emergency goose patrol. When a dog reaches the pond, the goose clears that lane once. Another dog getting through that lane ends the defense.
  4. Layer your squad. Frost stops jumpers from leaping. Storm Ducks arm for 2 seconds, then wait until a dog enters their splash area. Stones block duck placement, but dogs walk around them.

Promote your favorites. Press Upgrade duck (or U), then tap a defender. Pay 80% of its original cost for a Veteran: 50% more maximum health and 45% more shot or splash damage. Breadwinners bake every 7 seconds instead of 9; Patchducks heal 50% more. Damage is preserved proportionally, so upgrading is not a free repair. Emberbill burns hotter; Gustwing pushes farther; Penny Paddler earns +8 instead of +5; Brambill reflects more damage. Each duck can be upgraded once. Orchard Duck bakes every 12 seconds as a Veteran; Tempo, roots and toll rewards grow stronger. The strongest nearby Tempo applies, so a band of drummers cannot stack speed endlessly. Tap any placed duck normally to inspect health and its upgrade price.

Know your neighbors

Between waves you get a 10-second breather and 50 crumbs on Easy or the original balance, 40 on Normal, or 32 on Hard. Send the next wave early when you’re ready. Remove a duck for up to half its price, reduced by damage. Medals reflect how many of the level’s goose patrols you keep. Win a campaign level to keep its new duck and open the next level. Endless scores are separate.
