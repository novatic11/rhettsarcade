EXPEDITION 186 / COMMAND BRIEFING

Make a world of it.

Earth is a long way home. Turn eight pioneers and a cargo hold of supplies into an independent colony.

  1. Build a solar array and a mineral extractor. Mines need mineral hexes.
  2. Add a hydroponic farm and ice condenser to feed your colonists. Each person consumes 0.25 food and 0.2 water per sol.
  3. Build habitats to welcome new colonists. A research lab earns knowledge.
  4. Research metallurgy, manufacture alloy, then research and build an orbital port. Reach 24 colonists and launch with 40 alloy to win.

Power is capacity: if demand exceeds supply, production slows. Survey hexes for 5 minerals to expand. Dismantling refunds half the mineral cost. Turns advance only when you choose, unless Auto is on.
