# Real Solar System surface maps

## Solar System Scope textures

Mercury, Venus's surface and clouds, Earth (day, night lights and clouds), the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and its rings, Uranus, Neptune, Ceres, Haumea, Makemake and Eris: **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, [Solar Textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The maps are bundled locally at their original 2K resolution, resized at runtime for graphics presets, wrapped onto game geometry, lit and animated, and blended into original playable terrain. See [scope/sources.json](scope/sources.json) for exact file URLs and checksums.

Solar System Scope labels its Ceres, Haumea, Makemake and Eris textures as fictional. These are artistic reconstructions, not complete spacecraft photographs. Other maps also contain reconstructed missing coverage and adjusted colors, as explained by their creator. No endorsement by Solar System Scope or INOVE is implied.

## Additional spacecraft mosaics and original terrain

The following retained maps provide other moons' orbital imagery and original fallback assets.

Earth fallback: NASA GSFC Blue Marble. Reto Stöckli (land surface and shallow water), Robert Simmon (ocean color, compositing and shaded topography), the MODIS science teams and USGS. [Source](https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57752/land_shallow_topo_2048.jpg).

Moon fallback: NASA GSFC CGI Moon Kit, Ernie Wright; NASA/LRO LROC and LOLA teams. [Source and complete credits](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/).

Venus, Mars, Phobos, Deimos, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, Rhea, Iapetus, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Oberon and Miranda: NASA / JPL / Caltech / USGS, from the Magellan, Venera, Pioneer, Viking, Voyager and Galileo data/mosaics listed in [JPL's texture-map catalog](https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/). Exact download URLs and credits are recorded per image in sources.json. Missing coverage is blended with original procedural terrain; these historical mosaics are used for visual character, not scientific analysis.

Original albedo/fill maps also cover worlds absent from the selected texture pack and missing regions in historical monochrome mosaics. These are deterministic game artwork generated by scripts/bake-solar-surfaces.mjs. Known broad motifs inspire the designs; unknown detail is artistic. Terrain relief and the game distances/sizes are also stylized. Close-up materials, Titan's clouds and fictional gas-giant cloud decks are original shaders. NASA, JPL, Caltech and USGS do not endorse this game.
