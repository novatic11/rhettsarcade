THE FRONTIER IS YOURS / 1.0

Restore Cinder’s abandoned base, then build a working colony. Wire solar panels to drills, pipe ore into refineries, export alloy for credits, and turn gas into rocket fuel. Follow optional quests, research better equipment and plant your own base flags. Explore six star systems, including a red-disc black hole and blue pulsar. Wormholes link the stars, starting in an elliptical orbit around Brontes. Autopilot needs no electricity. Explorer assist offers optional landing checks and free recovery.
